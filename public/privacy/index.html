Privacy

Concept Card Workshop is local-first. Your workshop title, cards, timer preference, and any license token you paste are stored in your browser’s local storage on your device. They are not sent to Concept Card Workshop.

When you choose to verify or restore a paid license, your license token is sent to Sociobot’s billing verification service. When you choose checkout, you leave this site for Sociobot’s hosted checkout. Sociobot/Dodo handles that purchase under its own policies.

Projection links and join codes contain your card set. Anyone you share one with can read its contents. Do not put private student information in shared cards.

We do not use advertising cookies, third-party analytics, or student profiles. You can erase local workshop data through the Clear action or your browser’s site-data controls.
